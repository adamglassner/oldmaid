OLD MAID - A Victorian Parlor Game  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
================================================

Double-click OldMaid.exe to play. Nothing to install.

The game opens in its own window using Microsoft Edge (built into
Windows 10 and 11). If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC",
click "More info" then "Run anyway" - the app is not code-signed yet.

HOW TO PLAY
- One queen has been taken out of the deck, so the Old Maid card has
  no partner. Don't be the one left holding her!
- Pairs are two cards of the same rank and the same colour. They are
  laid down automatically.
- On your turn, click one of the face-down cards of the player on your
  left to draw it. Play passes to the left.
- When you run out of cards you are safe. The last player holding a
  card is the Old Maid.
- F2 = new deal, M = music on/off, F11 = full screen.

Choose 2, 3 or 4 players and the pace of play on the title card.
Your record (hands played, times safe, times the Old Maid) and your
settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\Old Maid.

All card art, music and sounds are original and made by the game.
