# Changelog

All notable changes to Old Maid are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added
- Play with friends: **Pass & Play** for 2 to 4 people sharing one computer. A "Pass to…" screen keeps each hand hidden until it's that player's turn, and computer guests fill any empty chairs.
- Play with friends: **Play Online**. One player hosts a table and friends join with the host's address, on the same network or over the internet. Everyone sees the table from their own chair, computer guests fill empty seats, and if someone leaves, a computer guest finishes their hand.
- New guests at every deal in single player: a parlor full of randomly drawn ladies and gentlemen (Lady Winifred, Colonel Ashby, Miss Primrose, Dr. Penrose and many more). Each has their own portrait in the game's hand-drawn style, a name and title to match it, and their own way of talking.

### Changed
- Old Maid is now a native Windows game written in Go. It no longer opens in a Microsoft Edge window, and it starts faster.
- The card art, table, music and sound effects are the same as before. Your record and settings carry over.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- First release of Old Maid: A Victorian Parlor Game for Windows.
- Classic Old Maid rules: one queen leaves the deck, pairs match by rank and colour, and the last player holding the Old Maid loses.
- Play against one, two or three parlor guests: Lady Agatha, Colonel Bramble and Miss Pettigrew, each with their own remarks.
- HD Victorian deck drawn in-house: top-hatted kings, lady queens with fans, page-boy jacks, and the Old Maid herself (with her cat).
- Green baize card table with candlelight, a lace doily for the discard pile, drifting dust motes, sparkles and smoke.
- Four original tunes on music box and parlor piano, with volume control and mute.
- Card sounds for shuffling, dealing, drawing and pairing, plus a dramatic sting when you draw the Old Maid.
- Choice of pace: Leisurely, Proper or Brisk.
- Saved record: hands played, times safe, times left holding the Old Maid, first-outs and best streak.
- Credits & Changelog screen and How to Play guide.
