# Changelog

All notable changes to Old Maid are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- First release of Old Maid: A Victorian Parlor Game for Windows.
- Classic Old Maid rules: one queen leaves the deck, pairs match by rank and colour, and the last player holding the Old Maid loses.
- Play against one, two or three parlor guests: Lady Agatha, Colonel Bramble and Miss Pettigrew, each with their own remarks.
- HD Victorian deck drawn in-house: top-hatted kings, lady queens with fans, page-boy jacks, and the Old Maid herself (with her cat).
- Green baize card table with candlelight, a lace doily for the discard pile, drifting dust motes, sparkles and smoke.
- Four original tunes on music box and parlor piano, with volume control and mute.
- Card sounds for shuffling, dealing, drawing and pairing, plus a dramatic sting when you draw the Old Maid.
- Choice of pace: Leisurely, Proper or Brisk.
- Saved record: hands played, times safe, times left holding the Old Maid, first-outs and best streak.
- Credits & Changelog screen and How to Play guide.
