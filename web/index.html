Old Maid

A Victorian Parlor Game

Pair your cards, pass them round the parlor,
and whatever you do… don't be left holding the Old Maid.

How to Play

One queen has been taken out of the deck, so the Old Maid card has no partner. Whoever is left holding her when every other card has been paired loses the game.

  1. All the cards are dealt out. Everyone lays down any pairs in their hand: two cards of the same rank and the same colour (two red sevens, two black kings, and so on).
  2. On your turn, draw one card from the player on your left. Their cards are face down, so choose wisely. Click a card to take it.
  3. If it makes a pair, the pair is laid down automatically.
  4. Play passes to the left. When a player runs out of cards, they are safe and leave the game.
  5. The last player holding a card is holding the Old Maid.
